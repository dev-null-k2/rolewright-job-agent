Your job search, done for you every morning.

Rolewright is an open-source AI job-search agent that runs on your own Claude subscription. Twice a day it scans about 270 job boards, writes a message for every role that fits, finds the recruiter's published email and tailors your resume without inventing anything. You work through it in ten minutes. Nothing is ever sent unless you click.

What it does

Everything a disciplined job search needs, in one private tracker that lives in your Claude account.

Finds roles

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, big-tech career sites, Hacker News "Who is hiring", Dice and staffing sites, plus your LinkedIn and Indeed alert emails.

Scores the fit

Every role gets a 1–5 match against your resume with the reason. Strong matches come first.

Writes from your facts

A short, specific message per role, follow-ups, referral asks, cover letters and "Why this company?" answers.

Checks every draft

A checker blocks any number not on your resume, cliches, location or visa mentions and wrong signatures.

Tailors your resume

One per top role, with every change marked against your original. One click switches back.

Finds the right person

Uses only recruiter emails the posting publishes, after checking the mail server. Nothing is guessed.

Today's 10

Your best roles one at a time: email, copy to LinkedIn, skip with a reason, next. Press F to start.

Watches your inbox

An hourly read-only check updates statuses from recruiter replies and drafts your answer.

Ask anything

Search, explain and update the whole tracker in plain English, with a pipeline view and a weekly review.

What it will not do

The guardrails are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Send anything for you

No email, application or message goes out until you press Send.

Scrape LinkedIn

It never logs in to LinkedIn. Hiring posts come only from public web search.

Answer work authorization

Those questions are always yours to answer, on every form.

How it runs

No servers, no API keys, no extra bill. Everything runs inside your Claude account.

The tracker

A private Claude artifact (app/rolewright.html) with its own small database.

The agent

Three Claude scheduled tasks that follow agent/RUNBOOK.md and the Python scripts beside it.

Your facts

profile.json, profile.md and RESUME_MASTER.md in a Claude Project. Nothing else is used.

Set up in about 15 minutes

You need a Claude plan with the desktop app, artifacts, scheduled tasks and the Gmail connector.

  1. Fill in your factsCopy the three templates in config/: profile, strongest claims and your resume in markdown.
  2. Create a Claude ProjectAdd the agent/ files and your three facts files under a rolewright/ folder.
  3. Connect GmailRead-only. Rolewright never sends, labels or deletes mail.
  4. Ask Claude to finish the setupPaste the prompt from SETUP.md. It publishes the tracker, creates the scheduled tasks and runs the first scan.
