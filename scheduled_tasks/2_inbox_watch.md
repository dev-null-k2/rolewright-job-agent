# Scheduled task: Rolewright inbox watch

Schedule: every hour, for example `CRON_TZ=<your time zone> 13 * * * *`.
Connectors: Gmail, Claude Code Remote. Attach the Claude Project that holds the `rolewright/` files.

## Prompt (replace <TRACKER_URL>)

Rolewright inbox watch (runs every hour). Read-only on Gmail: never send, reply, draft, label or delete anything.

1. Using the Gmail connector, run these searches:
   a) newer_than:2h -from:me -category:promotions (interview OR recruiter OR "next steps" OR schedule OR availability OR "phone screen" OR assessment OR "coding challenge" OR offer OR application OR candidacy OR "unfortunately" OR "moving forward" OR "your background" OR opportunity OR role OR position OR contract OR "W2" OR "rate" OR "submit your profile" OR "right to represent" OR "thank you for applying" OR "application received")
   b) newer_than:2h from:(ashbyhq.com OR greenhouse.io OR lever.co OR myworkday.com OR icims.com OR gem.com OR calendly.com OR smartrecruiters.com OR jobvite.com OR workablemail.com OR bamboohr.com OR oraclecloud.com OR hire.lever.co)
   c) newer_than:2h from:(teksystems.com OR aerotek.com OR actalentservices.com OR astoncarter.com OR roberthalf.com OR insightglobal.com OR randstadusa.com OR kforce.com OR apexsystems.com OR motionrecruitment.com OR jobot.com OR cybercoders.com OR dice.com OR collabera.com OR selbyjennings.com OR harnham.com OR wellfound.com OR ycombinator.com OR linkedin.com)
   Ignore newsletters, generic job-alert digests (the job scan handles those), marketing and receipts.
2. For each real recruiter, staffing-firm, hiring-manager or application-status email (read it with get_thread), upsert a doc in the `contacts` collection of the Rolewright artifact <TRACKER_URL> using the ArtifactData tool. doc_id = mail-<sender domain>-<sender first name> (lowercase, dashes). Fields: name, email (their reply-to address; skip no-reply addresses), subject ("Re: " + the email's subject), company, role (add "contract" or "staffing" if it is from an agency), channel "email", link (https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/<threadId>), status "reply needed" (or "rejected", "scheduled" if a time is confirmed, "info" for automatic confirmations), sentAt (email date), followUpOn (today), message (2–3 line summary: what they want, any deadline, rate or salary), reply (a ready-to-send reply, 30–70 words, starting "Hi <first name>," and signed "Thanks,\n<candidate first name>": plain, direct sentences; answer exactly what they asked; to schedule, offer days and ask for their scheduling link or two times; ask for client, duration and rate if it is a staffing role and they did not say; no filler such as "I hope this finds you well", "excited", "I'd love to"; never mention where the candidate lives, a city, state or time zone, relocation or commuting; use only facts from `rolewright/RESUME_MASTER.md` and `rolewright/profile.md`). Never mention visa or work authorization in the reply; if they asked about it, leave it out of the reply and start `message` with "WORK AUTHORIZATION QUESTION: answer this yourself." Rejections and automatic confirmations get no reply. If the doc exists, get it first and update with if_version; do not overwrite a status the user set to replied/closed unless this is a new email in the thread.
3. Keep `jobs` in sync: find the job doc for the same company and role. Update it with if_version: an application confirmation sets status "applied", appliedAt and followUp (7 days later) unless already interviewing/offer; an interview invite sets "interviewing" and interviewAt; a rejection sets "rejected" and rejectedAt; an offer sets "offer". If no job matches, create one (doc_id mail-<company>-<short title slug>) with company, companyName, title, status, found (today), url "" and why "From your inbox".
4. Watchdog: get `meta/scan`. If lastScanAt is more than 16 hours old, include "ALERT: the job scan has not run since <lastScanAt>" in your final reply.
5. Heartbeat (every run): get `meta/inbox` and update it with if_version (set it if missing) to {lastCheckAt: exact UTC time from `date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ`, summary: the first line of your final reply}.
6. Finish with one line per new item ("Reply needed: <name> at <company> about <role>"), then status changes, rejections last, and any ALERT. If nothing new and no alert, reply exactly: No new job emails.
