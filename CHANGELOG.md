# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.
- Twice-daily scan of about 270 job boards plus LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor alert emails.
- 1-5 match scores with reasons; strong matches first.
- Outreach, follow-ups, referral asks, cover letters and "Why this company?" answers, all checked against the resume.
- Tailored resumes with every change marked against the original.
- Published recruiter emails only, with a mail-server check; public "people to try" for big employers.
- Today's 10 focus mode, pipeline view, weekly review, Ask box and Apply kit.
